Which US state is closest in land area to today's country?

Today's country —

    A new country every day, based on the UTC date.

    How to play

    Every day the game picks one country. You have 6 guesses to name the US state whose land area is closest to that country's.

    After each guess you get two clues:

    • L or S: the answer state is larger or smaller than the state you picked.
    • HOT / WARM / COOL / COLD: how close your guess was, based on the percentage difference.

    Win by picking the exact state. Areas are land area in square kilometers.
